## What this service is

Members can create and manage a professional record, complete a profile over time, express interest in opportunities, optionally add business information, and — only after verification and publication decisions — appear in a controlled professionals directory. EXCO operators review records for verification and community operations.

Registration is **not** verification. Verification is **not** automatic publication. Publication is **not** the same as a member’s visibility preference.

## Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process:

### Account and registration

- Full name  
- Email address  
- Phone / WhatsApp number  
- Professional situation and primary profession / expertise  
- Optional organisation name  
- What you are looking for and what you can offer  
- Anti-abuse signals used during registration (for example CAPTCHA tokens where enabled)

### Profile and professional information

- Progressive profile fields you choose to complete (for example skills, services, location/community information, employment or job-seeker details where provided)  
- Profile completion progress (derived; informational)  
- Professional verification status and related EXCO review history  
- Directory publication / visibility status fields used by the product  

### Privacy / visibility preferences

- Your preferences for how allowlisted profile fields may be shown in directory projections  
- Preferences do **not** grant access by themselves; exposure requires preference ∩ product allowlist ∩ eligibility ∩ authorization  

### Business information (optional)

- Business profile fields you submit  
- Private verification documents you upload for business verification review  
- Business verification status  

### Opportunities

- Opportunity records created by authorised EXCO operators  
- Your expressions of interest in opportunities (a connection signal — not an application, messaging, or contact-exchange system in V1)

### Operational and security data

- Authentication session data for passwordless email sign-in (OTP / magic-link flows via the Auth provider)  
- Server logs and security/audit events needed to operate and protect the service  
- Role and permission assignments for members and EXCO operators  

We do **not** treat member claims as verified credentials until the product’s verification process says so.

## Why we use this information

- To create and maintain your professional record  
- To let you sign in securely without passwords  
- To support profile completion and member self-service editing  
- To enable EXCO verification, clarification, and publication decisions  
- To project eligible professionals into the controlled directory under privacy rules  
- To operate opportunities and interest signals  
- To protect the service against abuse and to meet operational/security needs

## Who can access information

- **You** — your own profile and settings while signed in  
- **EXCO operators** with the appropriate roles/permissions — operational views for verification, directory operations, analytics aggregates, and related workflows  
- **Service providers** that host the application and authentication (including the application host and the Auth/database provider used by this product)  

Directory visitors (including anonymous visitors where the product allows) see only the **controlled projection** of professionals who are verified and published, subject to visibility preferences and withhold rules.

Contact and certain community fields follow product rules that keep them private from public directory exposure in V1.

## Visibility preferences and consent

- Directory / profile **visibility preferences** are product controls.  
- A generic consent-management engine is **not** part of current V1 scope (PRIVACY-P1).  
- Visibility preference is **not** the same thing as legal consent.  
- This Privacy Notice is provided for transparency; joining or signing in is not implemented as a checkbox that records a separate legal-consent database flag.
