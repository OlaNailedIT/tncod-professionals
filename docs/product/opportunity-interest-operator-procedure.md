# Operator procedure — expressed interest follow-up

**Status:** procedure written for review. Human acceptance of the owner and the cadence is **NOT RECORDED**.  
**Capability:** current interest roster on `/opportunities/[id]`  
**Not in the product:** messaging, notifications, exports, applications, or introductions

## Human owner

The project owner is the human owner of this procedure. No named operator has accepted the cadence below. Do not treat this document as that acceptance.

## Proposed review cadence

During the controlled pilot, an EXCO_ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN reviews expressed interest once a week, and again before closing an opportunity.

That cadence is a proposal. It is not an agreed operating commitment until the project owner accepts it.

## Who may review

EXCO_ADMIN and SUPER_ADMIN. A MEMBER, a signed-out visitor, and an EXCO_VIEWER do not see the roster. Seeing the opportunity page is not the same as seeing who expressed interest.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open the opportunity from Opportunities. The detail page is `/opportunities/[id]`.
2. Read **Expressed interest**. Those names currently have an interest record.
3. Read **Potential matches** separately, when it is shown. A potential match is a structured comparison. It does not mean the person expressed interest.
4. Open the professional-detail link on an interest row. Contact review stays on that existing EXCO professional page. The interest list does not include email, phone, or other contact fields.
5. Arrange follow-up outside the product, in the way the community already contacts members. The product will not send a message, a notification, or an introduction.
6. If the member withdraws interest, their name leaves **Expressed interest**.
7. A closed opportunity can still show interest recorded before it closed. It does not accept new interest.
8. If the opportunity, profile, or account is gone, do not expect a row. Do not reconstruct the person from another opportunity’s list.

## What not to do

Do not export the list, turn interest into an application, or treat the roster as consent to be contacted beyond the follow-up the operator is already trusted to make through the professional record.
