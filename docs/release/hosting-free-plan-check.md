## Permitted-use clarification before unrestricted launch

Free plan identity is not blanket permission for every use. The
[fair-use rules](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines) restrict
Hobby to non-commercial use and include product/service advertising among
commercial examples. [Vercel staff have described unpaid nonprofit work as
permitted](https://community.vercel.com/t/question-about-commercial-usage/23321/4),
but that example does not specifically adjudicate this platform's professional
service listings and opportunity/referral use.

**Assessment: exact fit needs clarification, not an assumed violation and
not an automatic paid upgrade.** Preserve the current controlled scope; do
not introduce monetization or migrate hosting as an unapproved workaround.
