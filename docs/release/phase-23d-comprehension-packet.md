## How to run this (facilitator)

1. Sit with one real participant (member or EXCO). Use a phone or tablet if possible.  
2. Fill the Participant table with a **non-PII** label (e.g. `P-03`), role, device, date.  
3. For each row: ask them to **do the task** or **answer in their own words**.  
4. Write a short verbatim summary of what they said (not what you hoped they would say).  
5. Mark **Completed?** yes/no. Mark **Confusion?** yes/no. If confused, note severity S0–S3.  
6. Do **not** coach them toward product vocabulary mid-task. You may clarify after the answer is recorded.  
7. Repeat for additional participants as available. One filled sheet per participant.

### Success criteria (per participant)

Understanding is adequate when their answers roughly match the facilitator column for items 1–7 and 10 (registration ≠ verification ≠ publication; privacy/visibility; what happens next; how to get help). Items 8–9 strengthen confidence but alone do not override confusion on 1–7.

### Severity (only when Confusion = yes)

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| S0 | Believes join = verified, or verification = automatic public listing |
| S1 | Cannot find profile edit or privacy settings after guidance-free try |
| S2 | Uncertain wording but recovers with one hint after recording |
| S3 | Minor vocabulary mismatch only |
